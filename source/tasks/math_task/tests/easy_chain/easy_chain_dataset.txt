Let list_1 be the result of preprocessing task_1_input["result"] as follows - for every element, if it is a float, convert it to an integer by rounding ties away from zero (0.5 to 1, -0.5 to -1); then replace it with the remainder of its absolute value modulo 100 while preserving its original sign.

Compute the sequence [H_0, H_1, ..., H_{N-1}].
Let fwht_result_1 be this sequence.
Output the result as task_1_out = {"result": fwht_result_1}, replacing fwht_result_1 with its calculated value.

Task 3:
